Product Description

Emirates Emashield PU Wood Topcoat Matt (2:1) is a two-pack polyurethane-based topcoat, engineered to provide excellent water resistance and adhesion to wooden surfaces.

Recommended Use

This product is recommended for wooden surfaces for all architectural purposes. Used for interior applications.
