Product Description

Emirates EmaTex is an acrylic copolymer, heavy textured paint, designed to protect and hide minor substrate imperfections providing a decorative matt finish.

Recommended Use

This product is recommended for application on fibre cement boards, gypsum, concrete, masonry, plaster, and wooden surfaces.
Used for interior and exterior applications.

Key Information

Finish Matt
Spreading Rate 1.0 m2/ litre
Dry Time Full Dry: 48 hours
Application Roller

Product Features

  • Enhanced with advanced adhesion
  • Quartz and marble aggregates
  • Flexible decorative coating
  • Medium textured matt finish
  • Recommended for protecting and hiding minor surface imperfections
  • Weather resistant
  • Alkali resistant
  • Anti-bacterial, anti-fungal, and anti-algae
